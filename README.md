# Renew: 28-Day Bible Study

**Renewed Mind, True Identity & Healthy Relationships**

A free, interactive 28-day Bible study that helps you break negative thought patterns, ground your worth in God's design, and build life-giving relationships. Read each day's passage in three translations (ESV, NIV and TPT), alongside two companion books, and track your progress as you go.

🔗 **Live site:** https://okolofaith.github.io/renew-bible-study/

---

## ✨ Features

- **28 daily studies in four themed weeks.** Each day has a key scripture, a core focus, a reflection question and a Bible character spotlight.
- **Three translations side by side.** One tap opens the day's passage in ESV, NIV and TPT together on Bible Gateway, with a checkbox for each version.
- **Companion book tracker.** Checkboxes keep you on pace with *Battlefield of the Mind* and *The Power of the Mind*.
- **Personal journal.** Write prayers and insights for each day. Notes save automatically.
- **Progress dashboard.** See days completed, Bible readings, and chapters read in each book at a glance.
- **Two views.** Browse *Daily cards* by week, or use the *Reading tracker* table.
- **Light and dark mode**, mobile-friendly layout, and a printable tracker.

## 📖 The 28-day plan

| Week | Theme | Passages |
|------|-------|----------|
| 1 | Renewing Your Mind | Romans 12 · 1 Kings 19 · Philippians 4 · 2 Corinthians 10 · Isaiah 26 & Psalm 139 · Ephesians 4 · Colossians 3 |
| 2 | Shifting Your Mindset | Numbers 13–14 · Joshua 1 · 2 Timothy 1 · Matthew 6 · Philippians 4 · James 1 · Proverbs 4 |
| 3 | Rediscovering Who You Are | Genesis 1 · Psalm 139 · Ephesians 1–2 · Judges 6 · Exodus 3–4 · Jeremiah 1 · John 21 |
| 4 | Building Relationships | Ruth 1–2 · 1 Samuel 18 & 20 · 1 Corinthians 13 · Matthew 18 · Romans 12 · Proverbs 27 & Ecclesiastes 4 · Hebrews 10 |

### Reading pace for the companion books

- ***Battlefield of the Mind*** (Joyce Meyer): 1 chapter a day, Days 1–25 (25 chapters)
- ***The Power of the Mind***: 1 chapter every second day, Days 2–26 (13 chapters)
- **Days 26–28** are catch-up days, so you finish both books before the plan ends.

## 🚀 How to use it

1. Open the [live site](https://okolofaith.github.io/renew-bible-study/), or download `index.html` and open it in any web browser.
2. Tap a day's card to open the study.
3. Tap **Open all three side by side** to read the passage, then tick ESV, NIV and TPT.
4. Read the day's book chapter and tick it off.
5. Answer the reflection question in your journal and mark the day complete.

> **Note:** Progress and journal notes are saved in your browser on your device. They don't sync between devices, and clearing your browser data erases them.

## 🛠️ Built with

- Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in a single file, with no build step
- [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) for styling
- [Font Awesome](https://fontawesome.com/) icons and Google Fonts (Cinzel and Inter)
- [Bible Gateway](https://www.biblegateway.com/) for the scripture passages
- Browser local storage for progress and journal notes

## ⚠️ Notes on translations

Scripture text is not stored in this project. ESV, NIV and TPT are copyrighted translations, so passages open on Bible Gateway instead. TPT covers the New Testament, Psalms, Proverbs and only some Old Testament books, so a few Old Testament days may show only ESV and NIV.
